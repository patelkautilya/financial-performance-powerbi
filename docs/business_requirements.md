# Business requirements and review criteria

This specification defines the portfolio scenario. Stakeholder roles are proposed and do not imply client sign-off.

**Audience:** commercial manager and reporting analyst. **Decision:** investigate where revenue fails to translate into profit and define follow-up questions before changing pricing or discount policy.

| Requirement | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Provide a reconciled performance baseline | Sales, profit and margin cards on all pages |
| Reveal losses concealed by sales volume | Segment profit comparison includes negative Enterprise profit |
| Compare product and market performance | Product profit and country weighted margin visuals |
| Investigate discount patterns | Discount amount and margin comparisons by band |
| Support interactive investigation | Country dropdown updates KPIs and charts |
| Preserve analytical meaning | Chronological month axis, ratios of totals, source period and partial-year caveat |
| Let reviewers inspect the deliverable | Native PBIX, PDF, PNG previews, findings and source code |
| Distinguish inference from evidence | Recommendations are proposed and discount effects are not claimed causal |

The report is a historical sample case study. Forecasting, customer retention, causal discount optimization, live scheduled refresh and public anonymous Power BI sharing are outside this implementation.
